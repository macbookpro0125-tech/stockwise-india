# YouTube Shorts (12 Nov 2026)

Made by `node scripts/shorts.mjs` (needs ffmpeg): 1080×1920, 15–18 s, no sound. Figures from the live results pages, as the companies filed them with NSE. Everything sits inside the Shorts safe area (YouTube covers the bottom and the right edge).

How to upload: YouTube app or studio.youtube.com → Create → Upload → pick the file; YouTube treats a vertical video under 3 minutes as a Short. In the app you can add music from YouTube's library (it's free to use there). Paste the title and description below. One a day.

Never write "buy", "sell", "target" or "multibagger": figures, not advice.
