Daily results push for Jocular Insights (NSE stock research site jocularinsights.in; code in ~/Projects/stockwise-india, branch screeners-home). Read the last 20 lines of handoff/LOG.md first. Why: Search Console shows company results pages are the pages that reach Google's first page, so each day's new results pages are pushed while people search for them.

1. Run `node scripts/results-day.mjs` in the repo. It reads the live /results page and handoff/indexing-state.json and prints JSON: `google` (up to 10 URLs not yet requested), `bing` (up to 100 not yet submitted), `short` (a candidate company or null). If google and bing are both empty (between results seasons), write one line to handoff/LOG.md saying nothing new, commit it, and stop.

2. Google (built-in browser, the jocularinsights@gmail.com Search Console, already signed in; never type a password; if a sign-in page appears, stop this step and say so): open https://search.google.com/search-console?resource_id=https%3A%2F%2Fjocularinsights.in%2F. For each URL in `google`: click the search icon at the top, select all, type the URL, Enter, wait ~10 s; if it says "URL is on Google" skip it; otherwise click REQUEST INDEXING and wait 20–40 s for "Indexing requested", then Dismiss and wait 2 s before the next (clicking too soon re-requests the previous page). Stop at "Quota Exceeded" (about 10 per rolling 24 h). Record the ones accepted: `node scripts/results-day.mjs --mark google URL…`.

3. Bing (built-in browser): open https://www.bing.com/webmasters/submiturl?siteUrl=https://jocularinsights.in/, click "Submit URLs", click into the box and type the `bing` URLs one per line (check the box's line count with JavaScript before submitting), click Submit, confirm they appear as submitted today. Record: `node scripts/results-day.mjs --mark bing URL…`.

4. One Short, if `short` is not null, in the voiced style the owner chose on 13 Nov (Michael's voice, live site cards, see scripts/shorts-voice/README.md):
   a. `node scripts/shorts-voice/from-results.mjs SYMBOL` — reads the live results page and the company's public data, writes ~/Projects/files/shorts-v2/<symbol>-<quarter>/spec.json (the lines Michael says and shows) and post.txt (YouTube title and description, Instagram caption). If it says "not a two-figure story" (a loss quarter), skip the Short today and say so.
   b. Read spec.json and post.txt. Check every figure against the live pages (https://jocularinsights.in/stock/SYMBOL/results/<quarter> and /stock/SYMBOL). You may reword a line so it reads naturally, but never change a figure, and never use buy/sell/target words.
   c. `node scripts/shorts-voice/build.mjs <spec.json> --stills`, then look at the stills (contact sheet: `cd <dir>/stills && ffmpeg -y -loglevel error -pattern_type glob -i 'l*.jpg' -vf "scale=300:533,tile=6x2" -frames:v 1 ../sheet.jpg`): words must not overlap the small source line, nothing important in the right ~150 px. Fix the spec and repeat if needed.
   d. `node scripts/shorts-voice/build.mjs <spec.json>` (about 12 minutes; run it in the background) → <dir>/<key>.mp4.
   e. Upload with Claude in Chrome (the owner's Chrome, Jocular profile; if Chrome isn't connected, skip the upload and say so; the owner may need to open the Claude side panel there and sign in with the same Claude account as the app): copy the mp4 into ~/Projects/files/shorts-upload/ (delete it afterwards), open YouTube Studio for the Jocular Insights channel, Upload videos, upload via the hidden file input, then title and description from post.txt. No #hashtag at the end of the title (its pop-up swallows the next click) and never press Escape in the upload window (it closes it; the draft is kept under Content → Shorts → Edit draft); after typing the description click the "Details" heading to close the hashtag pop-up. Mark "No, it's not made for kids", Next ×3, Visibility → Schedule: today (or tomorrow if past) and pick "3:30 PM" from the time list (default time zone GMT+3 = 6 pm IST) → Schedule. No library music: the video has its own soft beat under the voice. Record: `node scripts/results-day.mjs --mark short SYMBOL`.
   f. Instagram (the owner asked on 13 Nov for the daily video on both): same Chrome window, https://www.instagram.com/ signed in as @jocular_insights (if it shows a login page, skip and say so; never type the password). Left bar "+ Create" → upload the same mp4 through the hidden file input (from ~/Projects/files/shorts-upload/) → OK on "Video posts are now shared as reels" → crop icon (bottom-left) → "Original" (otherwise it's cut square) → Next → Cover photo: "Select From Computer" with a still of the results card (copy <dir>/stills/l04.jpg into shorts-upload; the first frame is blank) → Next → caption: the INSTAGRAM CAPTION from post.txt (links aren't clickable, so it says jocularinsights.in) → "Add AI label" ON (Michael's voice is AI-made) → Share; wait ~30 s for "Your reel has been shared". Get the Reel's link from https://www.instagram.com/jocular_insights/reels/ (newest /reel/ link). Delete the copies in shorts-upload.
   g. Reels already made and waiting for Instagram (on that date only, post it with step f in addition to the day's Short — unless its caption already appears in the newest Reels on the profile; dates past are ignored): 14 Nov 2026 — ~/Projects/files/shorts-v2/bel/bel-q2fy27-v2.mp4 (caption bel/post.txt, cover bel/stills/l04.jpg); 15 Nov 2026 — ~/Projects/files/shorts-v2/sterlite/sterlite-q2fy27-v2.mp4 (caption sterlite/post.txt, cover sterlite/stills/l05.jpg).

5. Add one line to handoff/LOG.md (date; how many Google requests accepted; Bing count; the Short's company, its story, YouTube link and schedule time, the Instagram Reel link(s)) and commit handoff/LOG.md and handoff/indexing-state.json only (git fetch first; other tools edit this repo; never push; end the commit message with "Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>").

6. Finish with a 3-line plain summary for the owner (simple English; they read it on their phone). Never invent figures; no buy/sell/target words (SEBI); never publish anything but the day's Short (YouTube + Instagram) and any waiting Reel listed in 4g; never change account settings.
