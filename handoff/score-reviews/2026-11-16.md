## Later on 16 Nov: the EPS disagreements, looked into and fixed
The site's own share count (P/E, market cap, fair value) comes from the shareholding filing and was right. Only the caution compared the filed EPS with profit ÷ (paid-up capital ÷ face value) from the results filing, and that line is stale or in another unit in some filings: LTIMindtree's says Rs 10 for a Rs 1 share (10× off); CEAT 404×, Honeywell Automation 4,475×, Jubilant Ingrevia 1,580×. So users saw a false "the filed EPS doesn't agree" caution on those pages.

Fix (server/metrics.js `epsCheckFor`): no caution when the filed EPS is within 20% of profit ÷ the shareholding filing's count. Tests in server/research-test.js (a stale face value with a fitting EPS: no caution; an EPS that fits neither count: still a caution). Cautions don't change scores, so no score moved and RESEARCH_VERSION stays 1.13.

Result: 23 of the 47 cleared (LTM, HONAUT, IRB, KIOCL, FIVESTAR, CEATLTD, JUBLINGREA, VIDHIING, MCLOUD, PNBGILTS, PROZONER, RADHIKAJWE, GANESHBE, UNIVASTU, HILINFRA, XTGLOBAL, GEEKAYWIRE, AARON, DJML, CALSOFT, TRIGYN, VAISHALI, KALYANI). The other 24 fit neither count and keep the caution: real filing slips (e.g. SUMEETINDS ₹53 filed against ₹0.5; GICL, PIGL, JPOLYINVST) or a share issue in the year (AIIL, KISSHT, where the EPS is already re-based).
